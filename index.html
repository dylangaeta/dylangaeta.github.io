I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Cooperative Institute for Research in Environmental Sciences (CIRES), University of Colorado Boulder and the NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory. I am currently working with Dr. Aleya Kaushik on a NOAA/NIDIS-funded research project focused on drought and carbon cycle dynamics in the Western United States.

I recently completed my Ph.D. at Johns Hopkins University, where I worked in Prof. Scot Miller's Greenhouse Gas Lab. My dissertation was titled, “U.S. emissions of trace gas air pollutants inferred from a long-term atmospheric measurement network: from fumigants to fossil fuels.”
